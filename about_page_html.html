About Football Agency Sierra Leone

Building the future of Sierra Leonean football

History

Founded on Passion and Excellence

Football Agency Sierra Leone was established in 2026 by Alfred Manso Kargbo with a clear vision: to identify, develop, and manage exceptional football talent from Sierra Leone and provide them with opportunities to shine on both national and international stages.

What started as a small initiative by passionate football enthusiasts has grown into one of the leading football agencies in West Africa. We have successfully represented over 50 professional players and helped countless young talents pursue their football dreams.

Our agency is built on the principles of integrity, professionalism, and dedication to our players' wellbeing. We don't just manage careers; we build lasting relationships and support our players through every stage of their journey.

🎯 VISION

To discover, nurture, and represent talented Sierra Leonean football players, providing them with world-class management services, professional development opportunities, and pathways to successful careers in football while maintaining the highest standards of integrity and player welfare.

👁️ MISSION

To become the most trusted and respected football agency in Africa, recognized for developing world-class talent from Sierra Leone and creating sustainable career opportunities that elevate both our players and Sierra Leonean football on the global stage.
